## 1. Regulatory Compliance Boundaries

### 1.1 Third-Party Accredited Verifier Attestation
- **The Statutory Requirement:**
  Under Article 8 of Regulation (EU) 2023/956 (the CBAM Regulation) and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2547, starting in the definitive period (2026 onwards), all emissions declarations submitted by authorized EU CBAM declarants to the European Commission CBAM Registry must be verified and attested by an independent verifier accredited pursuant to Regulation (EC) No 765/2008.
- **CarbonReady’s Exact Role & Limit:**
  CarbonReady is a **pre-audit automation and compliance copilot**, not an accredited auditing body. It ingests messy shopfloor activity data, applies official CEA v20.0 grid emission factors, and formats the complete, standardized **Supplier Communication Dossier (Draft Annex III)** that European importers and accredited verifiers require.
- **Statutory Safeguard Implemented:**
  Every report and export annex generated by CarbonReady displays an explicit disclaimer:
  > *"DRAFT SUPPLIER EMISSIONS DECLARATION — Supporting communication dossier under Regulation (EU) 2023/1773 & 2025/2547. Not an official accredited verifier attestation. Formal submission to the EU CBAM Registry requires third-party sign-off."*
- **Bridge Implemented:** The application includes a dedicated `/verification` marketplace where exporters can dispatch their sealed pre-audit dossiers directly to accredited inspection bodies (e.g., TÜV Rheinland, DNV, Bureau Veritas).

## 2. Methodology & Data Model Boundaries

### 2.1 Complex Scope 3 Precursor Carbon Intensity
- **Industrial Reality:**
  In secondary Electric Arc Furnace steelmaking, embedded carbon in purchased Direct Reduced Iron (DRI / sponge iron) and ferro-alloys represents upstream Scope 3 precursor emissions.
- **Current Approach:**
  CarbonReady applies the authoritative Indian industry-standard weighted average factor (**1.75 tCO2e/MT**) documented by the Central Electricity Authority and Joint Plant Committee.
- **Limitation:**
  If an individual DRI supplier operates an exceptionally clean gas-based shaft furnace (e.g., ~1.1 tCO2e/MT) or an emissions-intensive rotary kiln (~2.2 tCO2e/MT), actual embedded carbon differs from the national average.
- **Mitigation Implemented:**
  CarbonReady includes an interactive `/suppliers` portal enabling MSMEs to request primary Tier-3 verified certificates from their suppliers. Until such primary certificates are uploaded, conservative published defaults are applied transparently.

### 2.2 National Grid Emission Factor Time-Lags
- **Authoritative Factor Used:**
  Central Electricity Authority (CEA) CO2 Baseline Database v20.0 weighted average factor (**0.716 kgCO2e/kWh**).
- **Limitation:**
  CEA databases are published retrospectively by the Ministry of Power with a typical 6 to 12-month reporting lag. Real-time regional grid variations (e.g. daily solar feed-in on regional DISCOM lines) are not tracked live on a sub-hourly basis.

## 3. Platform & Technical Boundaries

### 3.1 WhatsApp Business API Exclusion
- **Deliberate Scope Decision:**
  WhatsApp Business Cloud API integration was deliberately excluded from this prototype per competition instructions.
- **Technical & Security Rationale:**
  1. Setting up Meta Enterprise business verification and permanent webhook endpoints introduces external operational points of failure during live judging.
  2. Proprietary scrap-to-sponge charging ratios and monthly power sub-meter readings represent commercially sensitive industrial intelligence that factory owners prefer not to transmit over public consumer messaging platforms.
- **Alternative Delivered:**
  CarbonReady delivers a web-based, zero-installation voice assistant supporting live browser recording, audio file upload, and pre-recorded high-fidelity Indian Hindi/Marathi/English shift logs.

### 3.2 Client-Side Session Storage vs Cloud Database
- **Current Architecture:**
  Plant profiles, active batches, and historical report ledgers are stored in browser `localStorage` with reactive window event synchronization (`carbonready_profile_updated`, `carbonready_batch_updated`).
- **Limitation:**
  State is preserved across page reloads and browser sessions on the same computer, but does not synchronize across separate physical devices.
- **Phase II Roadmap:**
  An enterprise multi-tenant PostgreSQL schema with Row-Level Security (RLS) is architected in `supabase/migrations/` and scheduled for commercial roll-out.

### 3.3 Meltshop Ambient Acoustic Noise
- **Physical Reality:**
  Electric Arc Furnace meltshops generate ambient acoustic noise between 85 dB and 105 dB (scrap bucket drops, furnace arc hum, baghouse ventilation).
- **Limitation:**
  Live microphone recordings taken in close proximity to an operating furnace may suffer acoustic clipping that impacts Gemini 3.5 Transcribe speech-to-text accuracy.
- **Mitigation Implemented:**
  1. The platform displays an **editable verbatim transcript** before domain extraction, enabling shift supervisors to review and correct any misheard number.
  2. Pure manual text input and numerical form fallback remain available on the same screen.

## 4. Aligned AI Model Scope Boundaries

### 4.1 Strict Separation: Extraction vs Calculation
- **Model Role:**
  The deployed Nugen aligned model (`model_01m6tqnx1n5br0ca`) is responsible strictly for:
  - Industrial terminology comprehension in vernacular Indian languages.
  - Entity recognition and structured physical metric extraction (heats, metric tonnes of scrap, kWh electricity).
  - Missing-data detection and completeness scoring.
- **Strict Boundary:**
  The AI model is **never permitted** to perform numerical emissions math, apply emission factors, or compute financial CBAM tariffs. All math is strictly handled by the deterministic calculation engine (`cbam-calculator.ts`).
