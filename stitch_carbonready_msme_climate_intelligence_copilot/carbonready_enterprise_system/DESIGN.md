## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an industrial-grade climate intelligence workstation tailored for high-stakes exporters navigating cross-border carbon mandates (CBAM, BRSR, EU-ETS). The aesthetic converges at the intersection of high-frequency fintech terminals, mission-critical dev-tool ergonomics, and sovereign-grade infrastructure.

### Aesthetic Persona & Ethos
- **Precision Engineering:** Pure architectural utility. The visual signature avoids literal carbon cliches (green leaf graphics, earth illustrations, whimsical organic contours) in favor of high-density tabular data, micro-sparklines, hairline division rules, and razor-sharp metrics.
- **Tone & Demeanor:** Authoritative, vigilant, mathematically exact, and enterprise-viable. The environment projects the gravitas of a tier-1 financial terminal combined with the fluid execution speed of modern code editors.
- **Design Idiom:** Minimalist Precision Dark-Mode. Structural elevation relies on deep green-tinted slates, fine vitreous hairline borders, and subtle kinetic status glows. Every pixel serves auditability and compliance velocity.

## Colors

The palette leverages a calibrated dark-mode hierarchy rooted in obsidian greens, luminous emerald indicators, and an acid lime spark accent reserved exclusively for active delta calculations and CBAM liability alerts.

### Surface Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#060c0a`):** Deepest void background for overall application frame and shell viewports.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#0b1311`):** Base container canvas for dashboards, sidebars, and structural panes.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#13221e`):** Elevated data cards, interactive panels, and data table headers.
- **Surface Layer 3 (`#1a2e29`):** Popovers, dropdown menus, flyout drawers, and nested metric modules.
- **Surface Inset (`#081713`):** Recessed telemetry panels, terminal output strips, and active inputs.

### Brand & Functional Accents
- **Primary Emerald (`#10b981`):** Verified compliance status, audited emissions data, primary interactive actions.
- **Secondary Forest Deep (`#059669`):** Structural borders on focused components, secondary actions, bar chart baselines.
- **Tertiary Acid Lime / Electric Sage (`#a3e635`):** High-priority alerts, anomaly detection, CBAM tariff penalty flags, real-time sync pulses.
- **Luminous Mint (`#34d399`):** Accent line charts, hover glow strokes, active toggle indicators.
- **Warning Amber (`#f59e0b`):** Pending verifications, non-compliant supplier declarations.
- **Critical Crimson (`#f43f5e`):** Failed audits, unverified tCO2e overages, regulatory deadline breaches.

### Text & Border Token Hierarchy
- **Text High-Contrast:** `#f1f5f9` (Primary metrics, headline summaries, audit values)
- **Text Muted:** `#94a3b8` (Labels, column titles, taxonomy descriptions)
- **Text De-emphasized:** `#475569` (HS codes, unit labels, inactive states)
- **Hairline Border Regular:** `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)` (Default component boundaries)
- **Hairline Border High-Light:** `rgba(52, 211, 153, 0.35)` (Hover states, focus boundaries)

## Typography

The typography couples geometric sans-serif clarity with dense monospaced readouts to serve both rapid comprehension and auditable verification.

### Typography Hierarchy & Rules
- **Display & Headlines (Geist):** Handles macroeconomic aggregation figures, page headings, and module titles. Tightly tracked with slight negative letter spacing to project engineering precision.
- **Body & Editorial (Inter):** High-legibility humanist-geometric blend optimized for dense regulatory texts, compliance clauses, and verification audit logs.
- **Telemetry & Industrial Metrics (JetBrains Mono):** Mandated for all numerical units, financial conversions (INR, EUR, USD), Scope 1/2/3 calculations, HS Codes, EU CBAM CN codes, and facility timestamps.
- **Localization Directives (Hindi & Marathi):** When the application toggles to Devanagari scripts, body copy seamlessly resolves via standard system fallbacks (`Noto Sans Devanagari`) maintaining identical cap-height ratios, while metric badges, codes, and numerical equations strictly retain JetBrains Mono Western digits for international trade parity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an ultra-structured 12-column terminal grid engineered for multi-pane analytical dashboards, batch operational workflows, and data-heavy export manifests.

### Grid & Responsiveness Strategy
- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, `2rem` (32px) safe margins. Supports persistent left-rail navigation (72px collapsed / 240px expanded), central analytical canvas, and dynamic contextual side-drawers (420px) for transaction deep-dives.
- **Laptop / Compact Viewport (1024px – 1439px):** 12 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters. Drawer surfaces transition to overlay sheets; secondary KPI cards wrap automatically into 2-column or 3-column clusters.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters. Metric cards transform into horizontal scroll strips; primary emissions tables become horizontally scrolling data sheets with frozen identifier columns.
- **Mobile (360px – 767px):** 4 columns, `0.75rem` (12px) gutters, `1rem` (16px) margins. Layout collapses to single-column card feeds with bottom-pinned action docks.

### Spacing Philosophy
Internal card paddings enforce a consistent rhythm:
- Compact data tables and metric chips: `space-xs` (4px) to `space-sm` (8px).
- Operational KPI tiles, audit sections: `space-md` (16px).
- Major container viewports and regulatory report sections: `space-lg` (24px) to `space-xl` (40px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through subtle tonal layering and crisp hairline vitreous borders rather than heavy structural drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers & Hairline Framing
- **Tier 0 (Base Platform):** Hex `#060c0a`. Ground surface for root shells and gutters.
- **Tier 1 (Panels & Master Cards):** Hex `#0b1311` framed with `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Modules & Table Rows):** Hex `#13221e` framed with `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.18)`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Menus, Modals & Command Bars):** Hex `#1a2e29` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and `1px solid rgba(52, 211, 153, 0.3)`.

### Precision Glow & Accent Illumination
- **Status Glow Ambient:** Low-intensity diffuse emission: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`. Applied to active summary metrics and verified audit certificates.
- **Critical Anomaly Pulse:** `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -2px rgba(163, 230, 53, 0.25)`. Used on high-liability items requiring MSME exporter intervention.
- **Zero Heavy Black Shadows:** Never use opaque black dropshadows (`rgba(0,0,0,0.8)`). Depth is always achieved via contrasting surface luminosity and refined emerald edges.

## Shapes

The system implements a controlled, technical shape language (`roundedness: 1`) to preserve the feeling of calibrated machinery and industrial tooling.

### Curvature Tokens
- **Micro Radii (`0.125rem` / 2px):** Monospace tags, status indicators, chart hover tooltips, and table cell badges.
- **Base Components (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Standard buttons, text input fields, dropdown select triggers, and action chips.
- **Panels & Cards (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Primary metric cards, verification containers, and tabular analytical panes (`rounded-lg`).
- **Overlays & Dialogs (`0.75rem` / 12px):** Regulatory assessment modals, file ingestion zones, and slide-in CBAM audit drawers (`rounded-xl`).
- **Never Fully Rounded (Pills):** Rounded-full geometry is strictly prohibited across standard UI elements, except for circular 6px status pulse lights.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (Audit / Generate / Export):** Solid `#10b981` background, `#041f18` high-contrast bold text. On hover, background shifts to `#34d399` with `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary Action (Filter / Configure):** Dark slate surface (`#13221e`), `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)` border, `#f1f5f9` text. Hover shifts border to `rgba(52, 211, 153, 0.5)`.
- **Tertiary Utility:** Flat transparent button with muted monospace text (`#94a3b8`) transitioning to `#f1f5f9` on focus.
- **Critical / Anomaly Resolution:** Translucent amber/acid background (`rgba(163, 230, 53, 0.1)`), `1px solid rgba(163, 230, 53, 0.4)` border, `#a3e635` text.

### Monospace Badges & Chips
- **CBAM Classification Tag:** Background `#081713`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`, uppercase JetBrains Mono label in `#34d399`.
- **Emissions Value Pill:** Recessed dark background (`#060c0a`), unit displayed in `#94a3b8`, integer rendered in high-contrast `#f1f5f9`.
- **Scope 1/2/3 Categorization Chips:** Subtle color-coded pill indicators featuring micro-status pips (Scope 1: Emerald, Scope 2: Luminous Mint, Scope 3: Slate).

### Data Input & File Ingestion
- **Metric Input Fields:** Inset surface (`#081713`), crisp `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.18)` border. Focus states feature an instant border highlight (`#10b981`) and a 1px inner glow. Monospace font for inputs containing unit suffixes (`tCO2e`, `INR/MT`, `kWh`).
- **ERP / IoT Bill Ingestion Dropzones:** Dashed border using `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)` on `#0b1311` background, featuring drag hover highlight in acid lime (`#a3e635`).

### Data Tables & Grid Manifests
- **Table Headers:** Background `#0b1311`, bottom border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`. Labels formatted in uppercase 11px JetBrains Mono (`#94a3b8`).
- **Table Rows:** Alternating faint striping or pure hover highlight (`#13221e`). Bottom divider `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
- **Numerical Alignment:** All numerical values, emissions sums, and tariff calculations strictly right-aligned with tabular figures enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

### Cards & Telemetry Blocks
- **Emissions KPI Card:** Contains micro-header with taxonomy label, primary metric in Geist/JetBrains Mono (`metric-xl`), delta comparison chip (`+2.4% vs Baseline`), and an integrated sparkline rendered in `#10b981` without grid clutter.
- **Audit Chain Ledger Card:** Step indicator displaying verification stages (Factory Sensor -> ERP Extraction -> Accredited Verification Agency -> EU Customs File) linked via hairline emerald lines.
